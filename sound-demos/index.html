⚠

Demo quality on purpose

The sounds on this page have been made low quality on purpose: muffled, crunchy, with a little hiss. This protects the products from copying. The gestures you buy play in full, clear quality (44.1 kHz) in Second Life.

✎

All gestures are Modify

Each sound comes with a ready-made gesture: a trigger word (like /sg1) and a cute chat line, shown under every sound below. All gestures are Modify, so you can change the chat text, the trigger word or the shortcut key to whatever you like.

How: open the gesture from your inventory, click the Chat step, edit the text, then press Save.
